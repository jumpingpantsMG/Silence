privacy

Lock
Back to the typing test right now. Shortcut: Alt + Shift + L.
Lock when I leave the tab
Switching tabs or minimising locks it. This will also reset a game you were playing.
Auto-lock when idle
Locks after this long with no mouse or keyboard activity. Won't lock while a game or the soundboard has focus.
Open in about:blank
Opens the site in a blank-address window. Allow pop-ups if nothing happens.
Send this tab away after
When opening in about:blank, this tab goes to the panic page.

Drops what's loaded and fetches everything again.
