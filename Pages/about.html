about


Who

Who is behind Silence? Silence was created by 3 developers who wanted to create a simple, enjoyable, and free gaming experience.

What

What is Silence? Silence is a collection of browser games with a soundboard, forms, and a settings page for making the site your own. Everything is lightweight and loads fast .

Where

Where does Silence live? Silence lives right in your browser. There's nothing to download or install, and it works on any device that can open a web page. Games are served from fast cloud storage so they start quickly.

When

When do we add updates? Updates are added regularly to improve the site and add new features.

Why

Why did we create Silence? We created Silence to provide a simple, enjoyable, and free gaming experience without the clutter and distractions of typical game sites.

How

How does Silence work? Silence is a single-file html site that loads all its content directly in the browser using JsDelivr, you can find the source code at GitHub.
